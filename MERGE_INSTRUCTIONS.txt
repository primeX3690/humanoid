WHAT EACH GAP ADDS (the honest finding from each, not just "implemented"):

1. DISTURBANCE REJECTION (control/capture_point.py) - real Instantaneous
   Capture Point recovery (Pratt et al. 2006, same basis used on Atlas).
   Finding: recovery cannot undo the INSTANTANEOUS ZMP transient at the
   moment of a push (the support polygon hasn't moved yet), but DOES
   measurably reduce cascading violations in later steps compared to
   ignoring the push - a real, modest, not-magical benefit.

2. FULL RIGID-BODY DYNAMICS (dynamics/rigid_body_leg.py) - real mass
   matrix + Coriolis/centrifugal (Christoffel-symbol) + gravity terms
   for the swing leg's hip_pitch/knee_pitch, verified against EXACT
   analytic checks (parallel-axis theorem, physical-pendulum gravity
   torque) - not just "it runs." Replaces the earlier crude lumped-mass
   proxy in the torque feasibility analysis.

3. TERRAIN (terrain/terrain_profile.py) - footsteps can now be placed on
   real, non-flat ground (steps, ramps). Finding: stepping UP a 10cm
   curb works fine (within the existing leg-reach margin); stepping
   DOWN the same 10cm EXCEEDS that margin and IK fails, because the
   LIPM's CoM-height model still doesn't adapt to terrain - a real,
   documented, not-yet-solved limitation, not silently patched over.

Full docs/SCOPE.md and docs/BUGS_FOUND.md have the complete writeup of
all three, including exactly which test locks each finding in.
